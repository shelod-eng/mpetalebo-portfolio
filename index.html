How I Deliver

Discover -> Design -> Build -> Deliver -> Operate

Each stage is interactive and shows purpose, activities and artefacts.

01Discover

Understand the business context and operating problem.

Activities: Business analysis, stakeholder discovery, process mapping, capability mapping.Outputs: Business capability map and current-state assessment.
02Design

Shape the enterprise solution before delivery pressure takes over.

Activities: Architecture, user journeys, data, integration and security.Outputs: Architecture blueprint and delivery backlog.
03Build

Engineer the platform in controlled increments.

Activities: Platform engineering, APIs, applications and automation.Outputs: Working platform components with traceable technical decisions.
04Deliver

Move the platform into usable operating shape.

Activities: Deployment, testing, training and documentation.Outputs: Released platform with known controls and handover material.
05Operate

Keep the platform observable, improvable and evidence-led.

Activities: Monitoring, support, analytics and continuous improvement.Outputs: Operational intelligence and sustainable platform evolution.
