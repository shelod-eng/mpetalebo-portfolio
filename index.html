How I Deliver

Discover, design, build, deliver and operate

01Discover

Understand the business context and operating problem.

Activities: Business analysis, stakeholder discovery, process analysis, capability mapping, risk identification.Outputs: Business capability map, current-state assessment, future-state definition.
02Design

Shape the enterprise solution before delivery pressure takes over.

Activities: Architecture modelling, journey design, data design, integration design, security review.Outputs: Architecture blueprint, delivery backlog, design decisions and artefacts.
03Build

Engineer the platform in controlled increments.

Activities: Application build, APIs, data services, automation, dashboards and testable workflows.Outputs: Working platform components with traceable technical decisions.
04Deliver

Move the platform into usable operating shape.

Activities: Deployment, validation, user readiness, documentation and release support.Outputs: Released platform with known controls, owners and handover material.
05Operate

Keep the platform observable, improvable and evidence-led.

Activities: Monitoring, reconciliation, support loops, analytics and continuous improvement.Outputs: Operational intelligence and sustainable platform evolution.
